# Jellycat Market Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive market research dashboard analysing **Jellycat** — the British soft toy company that grew from a London startup to a **$450M global phenomenon**.

## 🔗 Live Dashboard

👉 **[View the Dashboard](https://siobhanM-dotcom.github.io/jellycat-market-research/)** *(update this link with your GitHub username)*

## 📊 What's Covered

The dashboard is organised into five navigable sections:

**Overview** — Company KPIs, revenue growth trajectory (2013–2024), and global plush toy market sizing ($15.1B in 2024, CAGR 8.4%).

**Global Markets** — Regional market share breakdown (North America 37.6%, Europe ~30%, Asia-Pacific ~22%), Jellycat's 41% US sales growth vs 2% market average, and the experiential retail strategy across London, New York, Chicago, Paris and Shanghai.

**Ireland Focus** — Ireland's $313.6M toy market, Jellycat's estimated 8–12% share of the Irish plush segment, the Síofra Shamrock exclusive (sold out in 27 minutes), and Brown Thomas/Arnotts distribution analysis.

**Product Categories** — 12 collection subdivisions from Bashful Bunnies to Seasonal/Limited editions, Google search trend data, price distribution (£18–£275), and secondary market valuations.

**Collectors & Demographics** — Buyer age segmentation (60.9% kids, 27% kidults, 12.1% collectors), the $1.8B adult toy spending figure, social media metrics, and mental health/comfort positioning driving the brand.
